Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Fix Your Elaka and how you can improve your neighborhood.

What is Fix Your Elaka?

Fix Your Elaka is a community-focused platform where residents can report local problems and follow their progress toward resolution.

How can I report a neighborhood problem?

Log in to your account, open the reports section, and follow the available instructions to submit a report.

Do I need an account to submit a report?

You should sign in to access reporting features that require an authenticated user. Available features depend on your project's permissions.

Can I track my submitted reports?

If report tracking is enabled in your account, visit your dashboard or the reports section to check the status.

How do authorities handle reports?

Authorized users can review and process reports according to the permissions and workflow configured in the system.

How do I create an account?

Use the registration page to create a citizen account. Authority registration is available through its separate registration page.
